# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient, and respectful.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Focus discussions on what is best for the project and its community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualized content, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through
the contact methods on their GitHub profiles. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove, edit, or
reject contributions and ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.
